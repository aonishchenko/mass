### The one sentence

> In MASS, a crew of verified humans builds an AI colleague together: its brain
> grows from their accepted contributions, every contribution is attributed on an
> immutable log, ownership is computed from that log, and when the agent earns,
> the money flows back to the people whose knowledge it used.

### How the four fit together

Each partner answers a different question, and none of the answers work alone:

- **World** answers *"is this a real, unique person?"* — without it, equity is farmable.
- **Hedera** answers *"can this record be trusted and can it pay?"* — without it, ownership is editable and unpayable.
- **0G** answers *"what exactly is owned, and did the thinking really happen?"* — without it, there is no asset and no proof.
- **ENS** answers *"who is this, and how do I hire them?"* — without it, none of the above is legible to a human.

Remove any one and the product breaks in a specific, demonstrable way. That is
the test we invite people to run on us.
